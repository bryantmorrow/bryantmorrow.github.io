Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This is a personal-use application that helps one individual, Bryant Morrow, manage his own eBay seller account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

Data accessed. The application accesses only the owner's own eBay seller account, in order to create and manage the owner's own listings.

Data of other eBay users. The application does not collect, store, or share personal data about other eBay users, including buyers' names, addresses, or contact details.

Sharing. No data is sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties.

Contact. Questions can be sent to bryant.morrow@runbox.com.
